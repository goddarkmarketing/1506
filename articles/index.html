FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kind of articles does the D&G Holiday blog publish?

Destination guides, luxury travel ideas, MICE and event insights, wedding planning notes, visa tips, and practical advice for corporate and leisure partners.

Are the guides useful for travel agencies and corporate planners?

Yes. Many articles are written for B2B partners who need destination clarity, venue thinking, and operational considerations — not only leisure inspiration.

Can I request a custom destination brief?

Absolutely. Use Request a Proposal or Contact Us with your dates, traveler profile, and goals — our desk will prepare a tailored plan.

Do you cover destination weddings and Indian market topics?

Yes. Wedding and India-market articles sit alongside travel and MICE content because they are core D&G Holiday specialties.

How often is new content published?

We add seasonal destination updates and planning guides regularly. Subscribe to the newsletter to receive new inspiration.

Can blog ideas be turned into a booked itinerary?

Yes — every guide is designed to convert into a real proposal through our travel, MICE, events, or wedding desks.
