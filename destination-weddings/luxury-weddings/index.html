FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Practical answers couples ask before confirming a date.

What makes a wedding 'luxury' in your planning?

Exclusive venues, elevated design, premium culinary, VIP logistics, and a higher staffing ratio for seamless guest care.

Can you arrange yacht or private villa weddings?

Yes. We plan charter logistics, safety, décor installs, and guest transfers for exclusive settings.

Do you offer private chefs and fine dining?

Private chefs, tasting menus, and sommelier pairings can be built into the celebration.

How do you protect privacy for high-profile couples?

Venue exclusivity, controlled guest lists, and discreet staffing plans are designed into the brief.

Can international designers collaborate with your team?

We regularly integrate overseas designers with local production and venue rules.

Is a dedicated wedding concierge included?

Luxury programs typically include a lead coordinator plus guest concierge support.
