FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Practical answers couples ask before confirming a date.

Do you offer partial planning as well as full planning?

Yes. Couples can engage us for full planning, venue-plus-coordination, or day-of execution support.

How does the wedding timeline work?

We build a master timeline covering vendor calls, setup, ceremony cues, and guest movements.

Can you coordinate overseas vendors?

We integrate your preferred designers or planners with local production and venue compliance.

Is guest accommodation part of planning?

Room blocks, welcome desks, and transfer schedules are core destination wedding services.

How many coordinators are on site?

Staffing scales with guest count and ceremony complexity — always with a lead coordinator.

When should couples start planning?

Six to twelve months is ideal for peak dates; we also support accelerated timelines when venues allow.
