Destination Overview

Europe with refined pacing

Europe remains a dream destination for culture, cuisine, and landmark cities. D&G Holiday designs European programs for Thai and Asian travelers who expect clear logistics, strong hotels, and elegant pacing.

From France and Italy to Switzerland, the UK, Spain, Germany, Austria, Greece, and Scandinavia, we shape private tours and small-group journeys that feel premium — never rushed.

Best Time To Visit

Best time to visit Europe

Season shapes crowds, alpine access, and Mediterranean comfort.

Late Spring (Apr–Jun)

Mild weather, blooming cities, and strong hotel availability before peak summer.

Summer (Jul–Aug)

Long daylight and coastal energy — book early for iconic cities.

Autumn (Sep–Oct)

Beautiful light, harvest seasons, and more comfortable pacing.

Travel Tip

Winter shines for markets and ski; summer needs earlier hotel holds for luxury inventory.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Practical answers travelers ask before confirming dates.

Do you design Europe trips for travelers from Thailand?

Yes. Itineraries, hotel styles, and pacing are tailored for Thai and Asian outbound travelers.

Can trips be fully private?

Private tours with drivers/guides are available across major European destinations.

Which countries are most requested?

France, Italy, Switzerland, UK, Spain, Germany, Austria, Greece, and Scandinavia combinations.

Do you help with Schengen visas?

We provide guidance and document checklists; final approval remains with embassies/consulates.

Are luxury hotels available in peak summer?

Yes with early booking — we recommend securing key cities as soon as dates are firm.

Can Europe be combined with Middle East stopovers?

Dubai and other Gulf hubs are popular stopover add-ons on long-haul itineraries.
