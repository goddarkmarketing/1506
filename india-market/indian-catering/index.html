Overview

Cuisine that feels like home

For India-market programs, food is hospitality. We coordinate specialized caterers and hotel kitchens for North and South Indian menus, vegetarian excellence, Jain and Halal requirements, and wedding banquet scale.

Whether the brief is a corporate lunch or a multi-night wedding, menus are tasted, timed, and served with cultural care.

  • Specialist PartnersCaterers who understand regional Indian cuisine at event scale.
  • Hotel IntegrationKitchen access and banquet flow coordinated with venues.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Practical answers partners ask before confirming a program.

Can every meal be vegetarian?

Yes — many India-market programs run fully vegetarian by design.

Do hotels cook Indian food in-house?

Some do; others host specialist caterers — we recommend the stronger path.

How early should wedding catering be booked?

Peak wedding months need early holds for kitchens and décor load-in.

Are Jain kitchens always available?

Available in key destinations through specialists — confirm at quoting.

Can you serve 500+ guests?

Yes with the right venue kitchen and service plan.

Do you provide alcohol-free beverage programs?

Yes, including mocktail stations and tea/coffee hospitality.
