FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Practical answers organizers ask before confirming a program.

Do you build booths yourselves?

We coordinate trusted build partners and manage timelines, approvals, and on-site quality.

Can you staff the booth with hosts?

Hospitality staff and interpreters can be arranged to match your brand tone.

How do you handle freight and storage?

We advise on freight windows and coordinate storage options with venue rules.

Can side events be planned during an expo?

Yes — dinners, press moments, and private demos can run alongside show days.

Do you support multi-city exhibition tours?

We can sequence venues and logistics when your show circuit spans destinations.

Is AV for product demos included?

Demo AV is scoped in the proposal based on your product storytelling needs.
