Overview

Groups that move with confidence

Large groups need more than seats on a coach — they need choreography. D&G Holiday runs corporate, government, school, and association groups with clear manifests, timing buffers, and dedicated coordinators.

From 15 guests to 200+, we design routes, hotels, meals, and activities that keep the group together without feeling rushed.

  • Dedicated CoordinatorsOne ops owner for manifests, timing, and supplier briefings.
  • Coach ExpertiseFleet sizing and route planning for comfort and punctuality.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Practical answers partners ask before confirming a program.

What is your minimum group size?

Typically from 15 travelers; smaller private groups can use our Private Tours desk.

Can you split large groups across hotels?

Yes, when inventory requires — with shuttle plans and clear communication.

Do you support government protocol needs?

We plan seating, timing, and hospitality touchpoints with protocol in mind.

How do you handle dietary requirements?

Collected in advance and briefed to hotels and restaurants.

Can groups mix meetings and touring?

Yes — common for corporate and association programs.

What happens if a coach is delayed?

Duty teams re-sequence stops and keep group leaders updated in real time.
